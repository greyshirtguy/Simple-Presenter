# Copyright display

The copyright of the song that is live, laid over its slides on the audience screens,
for the CCLI licence a church shows songs under, as ProPresenter's copyright display
is. Songs from SongSelect come with their details; any presentation can be given them
in the editor.

## Setting it up

**Settings → Copyright** has what ProPresenter has under General:

- **Enable Copyright Display**.
- **Show on**: the song's **First Slide**, **Last Slide**, **First and Last Slide**, or
  **All Slides**, as the presentation is played.
- **CCLI License Number**: the church's.
- **Display Layout**: the line, as chips in order: the song's details (Title, Artist,
  Author, Publisher, Copyright Year, CCLI Song Number, License Number) and the words
  between them (any words, and New Line). A click on a chip moves it left or right,
  takes it away, or has its words typed; **+** adds one. Under it is the line as it
  looks, made of made-up details.
- **Edit Template**: the editor, on the slide the line is drawn in. Its text box, linked
  to the copyright, is where the line goes and what it looks like, its font, size and
  colour, and anything else on the slide is shown with it.

All of it is kept in ProPresenter's own file of the workspace, Configuration/CCLI. A
workspace that has none starts with ProPresenter's layout (title, artist, publisher, ©
and year, and the licence number, each on its line), off; the file is made when
something is first changed.

## A song's details

In the [editor](editing.md), a presentation has a **Copyright** tab: its Title, Artist,
Author, Publisher, Year, CCLI Song Number and Album, and **Display Copyright**, whether
it shows them. A change is kept at once, and can be undone. They are the presentation's
own, kept in its file as ProPresenter keeps them.

## On the screens

As a slide of a presentation that shows its details goes live, on one of the slides
the display is set to, the template is laid over it on the audience screens and in the
operator's preview, its box showing the line. It goes with the slide: a slide that
does not show it, a cleared slide, or a look that keeps the slides from a screen. A text
box of any slide or stage layout can show the line too, linked to **Copyright** (see
[Editing](editing.md)).

## Guesses, and what is not here

- A detail the song does not have is left out with the words before it, so a song with
  no artist does not leave an empty line. Words only of spacing do not begin the line.
- The licence number is shown as it is typed.
- "First" and "last" are the presentation's first and last slides as it is played.
- The line comes and goes with its slide at once, not by the slide's transition, and
  the announcements' slides have none.
- A workspace with no file has a template of this app's own: one text box along the
  foot of the slide.
- ProPresenter offers the display only with "Show House of Worship Integrations" on;
  here it is always offered. Its CCLI reporting is not here.
