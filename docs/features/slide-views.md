# Grid, Easy View and Table View

The slides of the presentation being viewed can be shown three ways, as ProPresenter
shows them, picked by three buttons at the right of the bar under the slides, before the
buttons for the size of the thumbnails: four squares for the **grid**, a large **T** for
**Easy View**, four lines for **Table View**. The one in use is lit, and it is kept from
one start to the next.

- **The grid**: each slide drawn small, exactly as the screens would show it, with its
  group's colour round it.
- **Easy View**: the same grid, each thumbnail showing the slide's words alone, large
  and plain, over a colour, so that what each slide says can be read at a glance
  however its slides are laid out. Nothing on the screens changes. The slides
  themselves are not drawn here at all, which makes a long presentation quicker to
  open in it.
- **Table View**: a row for each slide, one under another: at the left the slide's
  thumbnail with its number, group and label, as the grid has it, and beside it the
  slide's words, and its notes, where it has any, in grey at the right. The size
  buttons, and Ctrl+= and Ctrl+-, make the thumbnails, and so the rows, larger or
  smaller.

Everything a slide does in the grid it does in the other two: a click takes it live
(anywhere on its row, in Table View), Ctrl and Shift select, a right click has its menu,
the live slide is ringed (the whole row, in Table View), media and actions can be
dropped on it, and the view follows the live slide as the grid does.

A **…** after the three buttons opens the view's **format** in Easy View and Table
View (in the grid it is dimmed, the grid having no format): the font, its size, colour, bold and italic, the alignment, and the colour
behind the words; for Easy View whether the slide's media is shown behind its words
(**Show Media**), and for Table View where words that do not fit are cut (**Truncate**:
at the beginning, in the middle or at the end). Each view has its own, kept in the app's
settings, and **Reset** puts it back as it was. They change only how this window shows
the slides, never a presentation or what the screens show.

ProPresenter's **Slides by Group**, which splits the slides into a row for each group,
is not here (the maintainer's choice). Nor is a key for Easy View: ProPresenter shows it
while ~ is held, and here ~ is [Simple View](simple-view.md)'s.

What is a guess, ProPresenter's views having been read of in its help and not seen:
how the buttons are drawn; the panel's controls, their names and their order, and what
each view starts with (Easy View white and bold on black, in the middle; Table View
light grey at the left, cut at the end); the size of Easy View's words as pixels on a
thumbnail 250 wide, made smaller to fit and growing with the thumbnails; that Table View
names a group on the first slide of each run of it, as the grid does, rather than on a
line of its own over the run; and that its notes are at the right of the words.
