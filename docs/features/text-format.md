# Text

**Text** (the letters Aa in the toolbar, after Search) opens ProPresenter's popover of
formats for the words of the presentation being shown: put on all its slides at once, or
on the slides selected. It is laid out as ProPresenter's is:

- **Font**: the family; the style (Regular, Italic, Bold or Bold Italic) and the colour
  beside it; **Capitalization**, as **Aa** (Title Case) and **AA** (All Capitals), the one
  that is lit clicked again for the words as they were typed; and **Size**.
- The alignment of the lines (left, centre, right, justified) and of the words in their
  box (top, middle, bottom).
- **Stroke**: the outline of the words, **On** or **Off**, its width and its colour.
- **Shadow**: the shadow of the words, **On** or **Off**, its blur, opacity and colour,
  and its angle, as a dial and a number, and its offset.

Each control shows what the words of those slides have in common, and is left empty
where they differ, as ProPresenter's are. Whatever is set goes to every text box with
words on those slides, and nothing else of them changes: their words, their chords,
their places, the slides' other elements and what the slides do stay as they were. The
slide that is live is drawn again on the screens as it now is.

The arrows beside a number step it, and step what each box has of it. The size's arrows
make every run of the words a point larger or smaller, from its own size, so that a
slide's large words and its small ones keep apart: what typing one size for them all
cannot do. A number typed is the same for all. Up and Down, and the wheel, in an empty
box, do what the arrows do.

Each change is for good, as Paste Text Style's is while showing. The first change made
while the popover is open keeps a copy of the file with the editor's backups, and
**Revert** puts the file back exactly as it was before that change, until the popover is
put away. A click anywhere else, or Esc, puts it away, and so does the editor, the
settings or Bible mode opened, or another presentation shown. It is not offered in the
editor, which has its own.

ProPresenter's popover has been seen in a picture, not used. What is a guess: that a
step of the arrows is a point; that Aa is Title Case; that the arrows beside the other
numbers step each box's own value where they differ, as the size's do; that a colour
given to the outline or the shadow while it is off turns it on, as the editor's colours
do; and the styles, which here are the four a family can be made in (ProPresenter's menu
likely lists each family's own faces, such as Light or Medium, which has not been seen).
Revert is this app's: ProPresenter has Undo.

## Selecting slides

A click with **Ctrl** held selects a slide, or lets it go again, and a click with
**Shift** held selects every slide from the last one selected (or from the slide that is
live, or from the first slide); neither takes a slide live. A selected slide has a blue
ring. The slide that is live counts as selected. A click alone, **Esc** (once the popover
is put away) and another presentation shown let them go.

Only the Text button goes by the slides selected, so far. (The blue of the ring is a
guess at ProPresenter's.)

## Apply This Text Style to All

This app's own. A slide's menu, in the show and in the editor, has **Apply This Text
Style to All** under Copy Text Style: the slide taken as a [theme](themes.md) for every
other slide of its presentation, as [Paste Text Style](editing.md) takes a copied one,
its text boxes alone. In the editor it is one step that can be undone; while showing it
is for good, and the file as it was is kept with the editor's backups first.
